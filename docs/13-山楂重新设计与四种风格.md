# HawTend：山楂重新设计与四种风格

日期：2026-10-05。状态：用户已选定 **G 细线手绘山楂**，搭配此前选定的 Lora 字体，已完成 [默认页面、安装图标与 EXE 统一](14-G品牌统一与桌面更新.md)。下面保留四套候选的设计过程与当时的验证记录。

## 这次解决的问题

用户指出 A2 的双果与长果梗像樱桃，果实上的五角星难以理解，要求重新设计并提供不同风格。A2 中的白色星形本意是花萼，但设计表达没有传达清楚；该图标不再作为当前待采纳方案。旧资产与截图保留为历史。

本轮以单颗山楂、较短果梗与分裂叶片组成轮廓。四套均保留 Lora 字体、暖纸页面与相同的虚构样例，便于比较画法与页面的融合程度。

形态参考：[Flora of China 的山楂条目](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=2&taxon_id=200010806)和[香港浸会大学药用植物图像数据库](https://sys01.lib.hkbu.edu.hk/cmed/mpid/detail.php?herb_id=D00086&lang=eng)。植物志记载山楂为深红色近球形或梨形果实，叶片有多对裂片；设计采用圆润果形与分裂叶片。照片搜索只用于了解形态，没有把第三方照片作为项目资产。

## 资产与生成方式

| 文件 | 用途 |
| --- | --- |
| [E 透明原图](../public/brand/proposals/e-botanical-v3-original.png) | 内置 image_gen 生成的原始插画，1254 × 1254 RGBA，约 1.48 MiB |
| [E 页头图片](../src/assets/brand-proposals/e-botanical-v3-192.png) | 原图等比缩小至 192 × 192，约 49 kB，保留透明背景 |
| [F 矢量图](../src/assets/brand-proposals/f-paper-v3.svg) | 原创极简色块标记 |
| [G 矢量图](../src/assets/brand-proposals/g-ink-v3.svg) | 原创细线手绘标记 |
| [H 矢量图](../src/assets/brand-proposals/h-print-v3.svg) | 原创单色版画标记 |

E 使用内置 image_gen，生成一张全新的透明植物插画，没有使用 CLI/API 回退。原图已复制到项目中，不依赖生成工具目录。页头版只进行尺寸缩小，未改变叶片、果实、颜色或细节；字体由页面单独排版，不烘焙到图片中。F/G/H 使用项目内原生 SVG 绘制，没有更换为位图。既有 Lora 字体文件与许可沿用。

对照维护命令：已有运行页面截图后，`node scripts/build-brand-review.mjs --styles` 生成桌面和手机对照 HTML；增加 `--marks` 只生成品牌图案对照。默认模式及 `--refine` 模式继续维护历史候选。

## E 的完整生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: transparent botanical illustration logomark for HawTend, a warm paper-colored personal life journal app.
Primary request: design a refined, quiet Chinese hawthorn mark in a natural colored-pencil and gouache field-notebook illustration style, original illustration, harmonious with a sage-green and cream-paper UI. One fruit only, clearly a Chinese hawthorn (Crataegus pinnatifida / shan zha), not a cherry or apple mascot.
Subject: one plump near-spherical matte deep warm red hawthorn fruit, slightly irregular natural outline, small scattered pale cream lenticel dots on the skin. A very short brown fruit stem, and one broad, deeply pinnately lobed hawthorn leaf angling gently toward the upper left; show 3 pairs of distinct leaf lobes, subtly serrated edges and a few understated veins. The leaf is large enough to recognize at small size. The fruit has only a tiny dark brown rounded irregular recessed blossom scar near its lower front, if visible at all; never draw a geometric star.
Style/medium: sophisticated simplified botanical gouache with a little colored-pencil grain, clean silhouette and restrained details, a small illustration for a quiet personal journal. A few broad painted shapes, subtle warm shading, no glossy 3D rendering, no heavy outline. Favor crisp readability when reduced to a 36px header icon over scientific plate detail.
Composition: square canvas, centered single compact fruit-and-leaf symbol, fruit-and-leaf silhouette about 78 percent of canvas, fully visible, generous padding. Short stem attaches to fruit; no dangling pairs, no long forked cherry stems, no detached leaves. Transparent background with real alpha, no ground or scenery.
Palette: muted brick red / cranberry red fruit, sage and olive green leaf, tiny cocoa-brown stem and blossom indentation; pale speckles. Warm and calm, not neon.
Text: none. HawTend lettering will be added separately using Lora in the actual app.
Avoid: cherries, two fruits, long thin stems, apple notch, heart-shaped fruit, five-point stars, white star cutouts, star badges, facial features, eyes, smiling mouth, cute character, arms, legs, open books, monograms, circular badge, border, surrounding objects, shadow on background, white background, checkerboard backdrop, mockup, watermark, lettering.
```
